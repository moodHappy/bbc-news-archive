German police ID Berlin Pride event suspect as Islamic State supporter

📅 2026-07-27 00:52 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

July 26 (UPI) -- German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt on Sunday identified the suspect accused of driving a car through a Pride event as Abdul Ballout, an alleged supporter of the Islamic State militant group.

7 月 26 日（UPI）——德国内政部长亚历山大·多布林特 (Alexander Dobrindt) 周日确认，被指控驾驶汽车参加骄傲活动的嫌疑人是阿卜杜勒·巴鲁特 (Abdul Ballout)，据称是伊斯兰国激进组织的支持者。

Dobrindt described the deadly assault as an "Islamist terrorist attack" during a news conference near the site of the incident in Tiergarten Park in Berlin, The New York Times reported.

据《纽约时报》报道，多布林特在柏林蒂尔加滕公园事发地点附近举行的新闻发布会上将这起致命袭击描述为“伊斯兰恐怖袭击”。

"Everything we have seen so far indicates that we are dealing with an Islamist terrorist attack," he said. "We know that the suspect, Abdul Ballout, is on the run.

他说：“到目前为止，我们所看到的一切都表明我们正在应对伊斯兰恐怖袭击。” “我们知道嫌疑人阿卜杜勒·巴劳特正在逃。

"This is a crucial point, because it means all police forces are engaged in a major manhunt."

“这是至关重要的一点，因为这意味着所有警察部队都将参与大规模搜捕行动。”

Ballout, 21, is accused of driving a vehicle into a crowd at Berlin's Pride festival on Saturday, killing one person and injuring at least 16 people. Police said one or more people appeared to have exited the vehicle after it rammed into the crowd.

21 岁的巴鲁特被指控周六在柏林骄傲节上驾驶车辆冲撞人群，造成 1 人死亡、至少 16 人受伤。警方表示，车辆撞向人群后，似乎有一名或多人从车里下来。

Multiple people also sustained stabbing injuries.

多人还被刺伤。

Dobrindt said there was evidence Ballout supported the Islamic State -- also known as ISIS -- and had a criminal conviction in May, The Guardian reported. Police called for help in tracking down Ballout, a German citizen, but asked members of the public not to approach or have direct contact with him because he may be armed and dangerous.

据《卫报》报道，多布林特表示，有证据表明巴鲁特支持伊斯兰国（又称 ISIS），并于 5 月被定罪。警方呼吁帮助追捕德国公民巴鲁特，但要求公众不要接近或直接接触他，因为他可能携带武器并且很危险。

"We are dealing here with a suspected perpetrator, who had previously drawn attention to himself through a high volume of criminal offenses, radicalization and affiliation with the Islamist scene," he said.

他说：“我们正在处理一名犯罪嫌疑人，他此前曾因大量刑事犯罪、激进化和与伊斯兰势力的联系而引起人们的关注。”

Police representative Florian Nath said the search was "proceeding at full speed."

警方代表弗洛里安·纳斯表示，搜查工作正在“全速进行”。

"We still have no information whatsoever regarding his specific motives, the exact sequence of events or his role in the crime itself."

“我们仍然没有关于他的具体动机、事件的确切顺序或他在犯罪本身中所扮演的角色的任何信息。”
