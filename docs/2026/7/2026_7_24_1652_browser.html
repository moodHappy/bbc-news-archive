Maryland man almost gave away a $50,000 lottery ticket

📅 2026-07-24 16:52 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

July 22 (UPI) -- A Maryland man almost gave away a scratch-off lottery ticket to settle a debt, but ended up winning $50,000 thanks to his wife's interference.

7月22日（UPI）——马里兰州一名男子差点送出一张刮刮乐彩票来还债，但在妻子的干涉下最终赢得了5万美元。

Cumberland resident John Hamilton Jr. told Maryland Lottery officials he bought his $5,000,000 LUXE scratch-off ticket for $50 from the Sheetz store on McMullen Highway in Cresaptown and put it in his back pocket, where he almost immediately forgot about it.

坎伯兰居民小约翰·汉密尔顿 (John Hamilton Jr.) 告诉马里兰州彩票官员，他从克雷萨普敦 (Cresaptown) 麦克马伦高速公路 (McMullen Highway) 的 Sheetz 商店花了 50 美元购买了价值 500 万美元的 LUXE 刮刮乐彩票，然后把它放在后兜里，但他几乎立刻就忘记了这件事。

Hamilton said he ran into a friend the following day and, remembering the ticket in his pocket, offered it to the other man to settle a $40 debt.

汉密尔顿说，第二天他遇到了一位朋友，并记得口袋里有一张彩票，于是他把它送给了另一个人，以偿还 40 美元的债务。

"I told him it could be something on it," he said.

“我告诉他上面可能有东西，”他说。

Hamilton said his wife stopped the exchange at the last possible moment.

汉密尔顿说，他的妻子在最后一刻停止了交流。

"She put her hand between us and intercepted the play," he said. "That's when I thought I should play it and see if it really was a winner."

“她把手放在我们之间并拦截了比赛，”他说。 “那时我想我应该玩一下它，看看它是否真的是赢家。”

The ticket turned out to be a $50,000 winner.

结果这张彩票中奖了 50,000 美元。

"I was dumbfounded," the winner said.

“我惊呆了，”获胜者说。

Hamilton said his prize money will go toward taking his family on a Disney cruise and paying down his mortgage.

汉密尔顿表示，他的奖金将用于带家人乘坐迪士尼游轮并偿还抵押贷款。
