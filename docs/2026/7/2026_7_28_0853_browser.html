US singer D4vd to go on trial for murder in death of 14-year-old

📅 2026-07-29 16:23 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

A judge has ruled that US musician D4vd will go on trial for murder over the death of a teenage girl.

法官裁定美国音乐家 D4vd 因谋杀一名少女而受审。

The decision came after a five-day preliminary hearing, in which Los Angeles prosecutors questioned a dozen witnesses and displayed graphic photographic evidence as 14-year-old Celeste Rivas Hernandez's family watched on.

这一决定是在为期五天的初步听证会后做出的，在听证会上，洛杉矶检察官询问了十几名证人，并在 14 岁的塞莱斯特·里瓦斯·埃尔南德斯 (Celeste Rivas Hernandez) 的家人旁观下展示了图片证据。

The star, whose legal name is David Anthony Burke, is accused of killing the girl, whose remains were found last September in a Tesla registered to his address.

这位明星的法定姓名是大卫·安东尼·伯克，他被指控杀害了这名女孩，去年 9 月在登记在他地址的一辆特斯拉汽车中发现了女孩的遗体。

Burke, 21, has pleaded not guilty to charges of murdering and sexually abusing her.

21 岁的伯克对谋杀和性虐待她的指控表示不认罪。

Burke is scheduled to be in court next on 31 August to have the charges against him formally read. The trial is to start within 90 days of that date.

伯克定于 8 月 31 日出庭，正式宣读对他的指控。审判将于该日期后 90 天内开始。

The charges include first-degree murder, mutilation of human remains and child sexual abuse. The murder charges include special circumstances linked to financial gain that could make Burke potentially liable for the death sentence.

这些指控包括一级谋杀、残害人体遗骸和儿童性虐待。谋杀指控包括与经济利益相关的特殊情况，可能使伯克可能被判处死刑。

Prosecutors have alleged Burke stabbed the teen before dismembering her body after years of sexual abuse.

检察官指控伯克在多年性虐待后刺伤了这名少女，然后肢解了她的尸体。

The accusation of murder ​for financial gain is among the special circumstances included ​in the charges making Burke potentially liable for ⁠the death sentence.

为了经济利益而谋杀的指控是指控中包含的特殊情况之一，使伯克可能被判处死刑。

They allege he killed her because the teen had threatened to reveal his relationship with a minor and destroy his multi-million dollar music career.

他们声称他杀了她，因为这名青少年威胁要揭露他与一名未成年人的关系，并毁掉他价值数百万美元的音乐事业。

Evidence read in court on Monday showed threats Hernandez made to Burke on 22 April 2025, stemming from arguments about his friendship with another girl.

周一在法庭上宣读的证据显示，埃尔南德斯于 2025 年 4 月 22 日对伯克发出威胁，原因是他与另一名女孩的友谊发生争执。

"I swear to GOD I will kill u", one expletive-laden text said, followed by a threat to strangle him and tell her dad lies about him. "i will end ur career and ur life I will damage anything," the text went on.

一条充满咒骂的短信写道：“我向上帝发誓，我会杀了你。”随后威胁要勒死他，并告诉她父亲关于他的谎言。 “我会结束你的职业生涯和你的生活，我会破坏任何东西，”短信继续说道。

The following day, Hernandez went to Burke's Hollywood home in an Uber he had sent to pick her up.

第二天，埃尔南德斯乘坐伯克派来接她的优步前往伯克在好莱坞的家。

Prosecutors alleged that Burke stabbed her once she arrived.

检察官声称，伯克在她到达后就刺伤了她。

During questioning at the hearing on Monday, Detective Corey Farrell of the Los Angeles Police Department said text messages indicated that the defendant and the girl had attended church with her family.

洛杉矶警察局警探科里·法雷尔在周一的听证会上接受质询时表示，短信显示被告和女孩曾与家人一起去教堂。

In response to the defence, the detective said he was aware the teen had misrepresented her age to the defendant, telling him she was 16 and 18 at different times.

在回应辩方时，侦探表示，他知道这名青少年向被告谎报了自己的年龄，告诉他她在不同时期分别为 16 岁和 18 岁。

Burke's defence lawyers argued there was not sufficient evidence that he had committed the crime.

伯克的辩护律师辩称，没有足够的证据表明他犯下了罪行。

They said her parents were aware of what was going on between them and had even given written consent for her to travel to London with Burke for a week.

他们说，她的父母知道他们之间发生的事情，甚至书面同意她和伯克一起去伦敦旅行一周。

The defence highlighted texts on the 22nd and the 23rd of April that suggested Celeste had insisted on going to Burke's Hollywood Hills home and that he had argued against her coming before agreeing to send her an Uber on the night of the 23rd.

辩方强调了 4 月 22 日和 23 日的短信，这些短信表明塞莱斯特坚持要去伯克位于好莱坞山的家，伯克曾反对她去，然后才同意在 23 日晚上给她送一辆 Uber。

He "repeatedly expressed love and care for Ms Hernandez after they broke up", defence attorney Marilyn Bednarski said in court.

辩护律师玛丽莲·贝德纳尔斯基在法庭上表示，他“在分手后多次表达了对埃尔南德斯女士的爱和关心”。

The medical examiner's inability to prove how the two penetrating wounds found in the girl occurred and the lack of surveillance video capturing Burke being home on the night of 23 April showed a lack of evidence, the defence argued.

辩方辩称，法医无法证明女孩身上发现的两处穿透伤是如何发生的，而且缺乏捕捉伯克 4 月 23 日晚上在家的监控录像，这表明证据不足。

Los Angeles County Superior Court Judge Charlaine Olmedo ruled that evidence presented during the preliminary hearing provided probable cause for a trial.

洛杉矶县高等法院法官查琳·奥尔梅多裁定，初步听证会上提供的证据为审判提供了可能的理由。

Burke is charged with murder, continuous sexual abuse of a child under 14, and unlawful mutilation of human remains.

伯克被指控谋杀、持续性虐待 14 岁以下儿童以及非法残害人体遗骸。

"The people have met their burden on all counts," Judge Olmedo said.

奥尔梅多法官说：“人民在所有方面都履行了他们的负担。”

She found that prosecutors met the threshold for the charge of murder with special circumstances, including lying in wait, motive for financial gain and eliminating a witness.

她发现，检察官在特殊情况下达到了谋杀指控的门槛，包括埋伏、经济利益动机和消灭证人。

Following the hearing, Los Angeles County District Attorney Nathan Hochman said his office only presented a fraction of the evidence it had against Burke, and that it would present everything in the coming trial.

听证会结束后，洛杉矶县地方检察官内森·霍克曼表示，他的办公室只提供了针对伯克的证据的一小部分，并将在即将到来的审判中提供所有证据。

Hochman also said his office had not yet determined if it would pursue the death penalty against Burke.

霍赫曼还表示，他的办公室尚未决定是否对伯克判处死刑。

Gruesome images of the girl's dismembered corpse were shown in court during the preliminary hearing.

在初步听证会上，法庭上展示了女孩被肢解的可怕照片。

A homicide detective testified that Burke had ordered a body bag, chainsaws, an inflatable pool and a shovel online in the days after Celeste's death.

一名凶杀案侦探作证称，塞莱斯特死后几天，伯克在网上订购了裹尸袋、电锯、充气泳池和铲子。

Prosecutors highlighted records that revealed Burke ordered chainsaws and a shovel to his residence after April 23. They allege he used a chainsaw to dismember Hernadez's body.

检察官强调了记录，显示伯克在 4 月 23 日之后向他的住所订购了电锯和铲子。他们指控他使用电锯肢解了埃尔纳德斯的尸体。

However, Los Angeles Police Department criminalist Lauren Wallace testified that the chainsaws found in his garage tested negative for the presence of blood and were not tested for DNA thereafter, a point the defence highlighted in their attempt to prove the evidence presented was inconclusive.

然而，洛杉矶警察局犯罪学家劳伦·华莱士作证说，在他的车库里发现的电锯的血迹检测呈阴性，此后也没有进行 DNA 检测，辩方在试图证明所提供的证据时强调的这一点是不确定的。

However, investigators were unable to locate a third, 10-inch Makita chainsaw, which prosecutors claim Burke also ordered following Hernandez's death.

然而，调查人员无法找到第三把 10 英寸的牧田电锯，检察官称伯克在埃尔南德斯死后也订购了这把电锯。
