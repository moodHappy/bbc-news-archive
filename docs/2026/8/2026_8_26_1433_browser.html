Dolly Parton: Life of the music legend whose songs touched millions
多莉·帕顿：感动数百万人的音乐传奇的一生

📅 2026-08-26 14:29 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

Dolly Parton, the country singer from the Smoky Mountains of Tennessee, will be remembered for her songs that told beautiful stories so honestly, such as Coat of Many Colors, Jolene and I Will Always Love You.

来自田纳西州大烟山的乡村歌手多莉·帕顿，将因其真诚讲述美丽故事的歌曲而被人们铭记，例如《多采多姿的外套》（Coat of Many Colors）、《乔琳》（Jolene）和《我将永远爱你》（I Will Always Love You）。

In a seven-decade career, Parton won 10 Grammy awards, sold more than 100 million records worldwide, and wrote in excess of 3,000 songs.

在七十年的职业生涯中，帕顿赢得了10项格莱美奖，在全球售出超过1亿张唱片，并创作了3000多首歌曲。

Parton took her writing seriously, and considered herself a songwriter over everything else. "It's my way of expressing myself," she said. "It's my therapy.”

帕顿认真对待自己的创作，并认为自己首先是一位词曲作者。“这是我表达自己的方式，”她说。“这是我的疗法。”

She was also a savvy businesswoman who wasn't afraid to make tough decisions. Armed with a quick wit and southern charm, she took control of her showbusiness career that spanned more than 60 years.

她也是一位精明的女商人，不怕做出艰难的决定。凭借敏捷的才智和南方的魅力，她掌控了自己长达60多年的演艺生涯。

Dolly Rebecca Parton was born in 1946, the fourth of 12 children, into a poor farming family who lived in a one-bedroom cabin in Tennessee.

多莉·丽贝卡·帕顿出生于1946年，在12个孩子中排行老四，出生在田纳西州一个贫穷的农业家庭，住在一间单卧室的木屋里。

Her father, Robert Lee Parton, was a tobacco farmer and paid a missionary a sack of cornmeal for delivering her.

她的父亲罗伯特·李·帕顿是一位烟草农民，曾用一袋玉米面付给传教士作为接生她的报酬。

There was already music in the family - her mother, Avie Lee, sang folk songs all the time and her aunt played the guitar and wrote songs.

她的家庭充满了音乐氛围——她的母亲阿维·李经常唱民歌，她的阿姨弹吉他并写歌。

During some of Parton's earliest performances, her uncle, Bill Owens, accompanied her on the guitar - formative years for the future music legend.

在帕顿最早的一些表演中，她的叔叔比尔·欧文斯用吉他为她伴奏——这是这位未来音乐传奇的形成岁月。

From the age of six, she sang in the local church where her grandfather was the pastor, and was performing on her local TV station by the time she was a teenager.

从六岁起，她就在她祖父担任牧师的当地教堂里唱歌，到了十几岁时，她已经开始在当地的电视台表演。

In 1959, when she was 13 years old, Parton performed with Uncle Bill at the Grand Ole Opry in Nashville - a live broadcast that is regarded as country music's biggest stage.

1959年，13岁的帕顿和比尔叔叔在纳什维尔的“大奥普里”（Grand Ole Opry）登台演出——这场现场直播被视为乡村音乐的最高舞台。

She was introduced by Johnny Cash and received three encores.

她由约翰尼·卡什（Johnny Cash）介绍出场，并获得了三次返场。

The 'town tramp'
镇上的“荡妇”

During her teenage years, Parton began to model herself on a local character who was known as the “town tramp”.

在她的少女时代，帕顿开始模仿当地一个被称为“镇上荡妇”的人物。

The lady wore bright red nail polish, low-cut tops and high heels - young Dolly thought she was beautiful.

那位女士涂着鲜红的指甲油，穿着低胸上衣和高跟鞋——年轻的多莉觉得她很美。

When people said to her: “She ain’t nothing but trash”, Parton would reply: "Well, that’s what I’m gonna be when I grow up”.

当人们对她说：“她不过是个垃圾”时，帕顿会回答：“好吧，那就是我长大后想成为的样子。”

Parton sang “I’m just a backwoods Barbie / too much make-up, too much hair”, but her image was both a coat of armour and an astute disguise that helped get her noticed in showbusiness.

帕顿唱道“我只是一个乡巴佬芭比 / 浓妆艳抹，头发做作”，但她的形象既是一件盔甲，也是一种精明的伪装，帮助她在演艺界受到瞩目。

She would get ahead of the jokes by making them first, and had a quick and simple response for anyone who suggested she should tone it down: "Go to hell."

她总是先发制人地开自己的玩笑，对于任何建议她收敛一点的人，她都有一个简单直接的回答：“去死吧。”

After graduating from high school in 1964, she moved to Nashville to launch her singing career and met her future husband, Carl Dean, on her very first day.

1964年高中毕业后，她搬到纳什维尔开始她的歌唱生涯，并在第一天就遇到了她未来的丈夫卡尔·迪恩。

They married two years later and remained together until his death in 2025.

两年后他们结婚，并一直相伴直到他2025年去世。

In Nashville, Parton's singing caught the eye of country performer Porter Wagoner, who hired her to perform on his television programme.

在纳什维尔，帕顿的歌声吸引了乡村歌手波特·瓦格纳（Porter Wagoner）的注意，他聘请她在他的电视节目中表演。

Over the next seven years, the pair became one of country music's most popular duos, releasing so many duets that many fans assumed they were a couple.

在接下来的七年里，这对搭档成为了乡村音乐界最受欢迎的二重唱之一，发行了许多对唱歌曲，以至于许多歌迷以为他们是一对情侣。

But their relationship was a complicated one, and as Parton's solo career started to rise, so did tensions behind the scenes.

但他们的关系很复杂，随着帕顿的单飞生涯开始起步，幕后的紧张局势也随之加剧。

Writing in her book Star of the Show: My Life on Stage, Parton said Wagoner saw her success away from his show as a "threat".

帕顿在她的书《节目之星：我的舞台生涯》中写道，瓦格纳将她在他的节目之外取得的成功视为一种“威胁”。

While travelling with Wagoner on a tour bus in 1969, Parton wrote Coat of Many Colors, a beautifully emotive tale of how she grew up poor, but rich with love.

1969年，在与瓦格纳乘坐巡演大巴旅行时，帕顿写下了《多采多姿的外套》（Coat of Many Colors），这是一个关于她成长于贫困但充满爱的感人故事。

Scrawled on the back of one of Wagoner's dry cleaning receipts, it was rated by Rolling Stone magazine as her second best-ever song. Years later, Parton named her own children's book Coat of Many Colors, and it was the name of a 2015 TV movie based on her life.

这首歌潦草地写在瓦格纳的一张干洗收据背面，被《滚石》杂志评为她有史以来第二好的歌曲。多年后，帕顿将自己的童书命名为《多采多姿的外套》，这也是一部2015年基于她生活的电视电影的名字。

"It's a song that's just stood by me... it's created a life of many colours for me," she said.

“这是一首一直陪伴着我的歌……它为我创造了多姿多彩的生活，”她说。

'Sad little country song'
“悲伤的乡村小调”

Despite being unable to read sheet music, Parton played several instruments including the banjo, harp, piano and saxophone.

尽管看不懂乐谱，帕顿却会演奏多种乐器，包括班卓琴、竖琴、钢琴和萨克斯管。

She had her first number one hit - and first Grammy nomination - with the song Joshua in 1971 and finally left The Porter Wagoner Show in 1974 to concentrate on her solo career.

1971年，她凭借歌曲《约书亚》（Joshua）获得了她的第一首冠军单曲和第一次格莱美提名，最终在1974年离开了《波特·瓦格纳秀》，专注于她的单飞生涯。

To say goodbye to him, she wrote I Will Always Love You, a song that many think is about two lovers parting ways.

为了向他告别，她写了《我将永远爱你》（I Will Always Love You），这首许多人认为是关于两个恋人分手的歌。

"That's the prettiest song I ever heard," Wagoner cried when he first heard it, her website says.

她的网站上说，当瓦格纳第一次听到这首歌时，他哭着说：“这是我听过的最美的歌。”

The same year, Elvis Presley was set to record a cover of the song, but Parton pulled the deal when his manager demanded half of the songwriting rights.

同一年，猫王（Elvis Presley）准备录制这首歌的翻唱版本，但当他的经纪人要求获得一半的词曲版权时，帕顿取消了这笔交易。

She cried all night at the difficult decision, and Priscilla Presley later told her that Elvis sang the song to her during their divorce.

为了这个艰难的决定，她哭了一整夜。后来普莉希拉·普雷斯利（Priscilla Presley）告诉她，在他们离婚期间，猫王曾对她唱过这首歌。

In 1992, Whitney Houston transformed it into a now-iconic power ballad.

1992年，惠特妮·休斯顿（Whitney Houston）将其演绎成了一首如今标志性的震撼情歌。

Parton said she was driving when she first heard the cover and had to pull over: “When I heard her go from the a cappella into the music part, I honestly thought I was going to have a heart attack, it was one of the most overwhelming things.”

帕顿说，她第一次听到这个翻唱版本时正在开车，不得不把车停在路边：“当我听到她从无伴奏合唱进入音乐部分时，我真的以为自己心脏病要发作了，那是压倒一切的情感体验之一。”

Houston's cover of Parton's “sad little country song” was one of the proudest moments of her life, she said.

她说，休斯顿对帕顿这首“悲伤的乡村小调”的翻唱，是她生命中最自豪的时刻之一。

Parton re-recorded the song for The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas, a 1982 film in which she starred and was nominated for a Golden Globe for best actress.

帕顿为1982年由她主演的电影《德州巴黎春梦》（The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas）重新录制了这首歌，并因此获得了金球奖最佳女主角提名。

That other woman
那个女人

Another of her most famous songs was released in 1974 - Jolene.

她另一首最著名的歌曲发行于1974年——《乔琳》（Jolene）。

Showing her aching vulnerability, it's about a bank clerk with flaming locks of auburn hair who flirted with her husband.

这首歌展现了她痛苦的脆弱，讲述了一个留着火红色头发的银行职员与她丈夫调情的故事。

"Jolene, I'm begging of you, please don't take my man," she pleads.

“乔琳，我求求你，请不要抢走我的男人，”她恳求道。

Parton said her husband, who was notoriously private, was a bit embarrassed by the song. But it was nothing serious - just a running joke between them: "I was saying, 'Hell, you're spending a lot of time at the bank. I don't believe we've got that kind of money.'"

帕顿说，她那位出了名注重隐私的丈夫对这首歌感到有点尴尬。但这没什么大不了的，只是他们之间的一个玩笑：“我说，‘见鬼，你在银行花的时间也太多了。我不相信我们有那么多钱。’”

The song has been covered by many artists including The White Stripes, Miley Cyrus and Beyoncé, who changed up the lyrics to the more ominous: "I'm warning you, don't come for my man."

这首歌被许多艺术家翻唱过，包括白色条纹乐队（The White Stripes）、麦莉·赛勒斯（Miley Cyrus）和碧昂丝（Beyoncé），后者将歌词改得更具警告意味：“我警告你，别来惹我的男人。”

In the years to come, Dolly Parton's success only grew, with more hits, nominations and awards - she won her first Grammy in 1978 for Here You Come Again.

在接下来的岁月里，多莉·帕顿的成功与日俱增，获得了更多的热门单曲、提名和奖项——1978年，她凭借《Here You Come Again》赢得了她的第一座格莱美奖。

Playing her acrylic nails
弹奏她的水晶指甲

It was the theme song that Dolly Parton wrote for the 1980 movie 9 to 5 that fully propelled her to stardom.

正是多莉·帕顿为1980年电影《朝九晚五》（9 to 5）创作的主题曲，将她彻底推向了明星的宝座。

Actress Jane Fonda, who produced and starred in the film about women being discriminated against in the workplace, made a deal with Parton - she'd star in the film if she could also write the theme song.

这部电影讲述了女性在工作场所受到歧视的故事，由女演员简·方达（Jane Fonda）制作并主演。方达与帕顿达成了一项协议——如果她也能创作主题曲，她就会出演这部电影。

Realising her nails clicking together sounded like a typewriter, she wrote 9 to 5 while waiting around on set. Fonda said she realised straight away that it wasn't just a theme song - it was an anthem.

意识到自己指甲敲击的声音听起来像打字机后，她在片场等待时写下了《朝九晚五》。方达说，她立刻意识到这不仅仅是一首主题曲——它是一首赞歌。

The film was a big success at the box office and the song won several awards, including a Golden Globe.

这部电影在票房上取得了巨大成功，这首歌也赢得了包括金球奖在内的多个奖项。

'I look like a woman but I think like a man'
“我看起来像个女人，但我的思维像个男人”

Parton was a successful and shrewd businesswoman, and in 2022 was listed by Forbes as one of America's richest self-made women.

帕顿是一位成功且精明的女商人，在2022年被《福布斯》列为美国最富有的白手起家女性之一。

"I look like a woman but I think like a man. I've done business with men who think I'm as silly as I look. By the time they realise I'm not, I've got the money and gone," she once said.

她曾说过：“我看起来像个女人，但我的思维像个男人。我曾和那些认为我像外表一样愚蠢的男人做生意。等他们意识到我并不傻时，我已经拿着钱走了。”

Her physical appearance - the costumes, wigs, long painted nails and high heels - were merely a facade of a stereotypical “dumb blonde”. But she always said she knew she wasn't dumb "and I also know that I'm not blonde".

她的外表——华丽的服装、假发、长长的彩绘指甲和高跟鞋——仅仅是一种典型的“无脑金发女郎”的伪装。但她总是说她知道自己不笨，“我也知道我不是金发女郎”。

She had her own record label, Dolly Records, and co-owned The Dollywood Company, which manages entertainment venues, a theme park, a water park and a resort.

她拥有自己的唱片公司Dolly Records，并共同拥有多莉坞公司（The Dollywood Company），该公司管理着娱乐场所、一个主题公园、一个水上乐园和一个度假村。

In 1986, she opened her Dollywood amusement park in the foothills of her beloved Smoky Mountains. As well as rollercoasters, it is home to the largest sanctuary for non-releasable bald eagles in the world. Its rehabilitation and breeding work has won awards and in 2007, the bald eagle was removed from the endangered species list.

1986年，她在她钟爱的大烟山山脚下开设了多莉坞游乐园。除了过山车，这里还是世界上最大的无法放归自然的白头海雕保护区。其康复和繁殖工作屡获殊荣，2007年，白头海雕被从濒危物种名单中移除。

Despite her wealth and success, Parton remained humble and supported the communities where she grew up.

尽管拥有财富和成功，帕顿仍然保持谦逊，并支持她长大的社区。

Inspired by her father who couldn’t read or write, she launched the Imagination Library in 1999, which gave free books to children in Tennessee. It has since expanded to Australia, the UK, Ireland and Canada, sending out millions of free books every year.

受到不识字的父亲的启发，她于1999年创办了“想象力图书馆”（Imagination Library），免费向田纳西州的儿童赠书。该项目此后扩展到澳大利亚、英国、爱尔兰和加拿大，每年送出数百万本免费书籍。

Her non-profit organisation, The Dollywood Foundation, has funded scholarships, hospitals and donated to flood and wildfire victims.

她的非营利组织多莉坞基金会资助了奖学金、医院，并向洪水和野火的受害者捐款。

In 2020, as the Covid-19 pandemic gripped the world, Parton donated $1m (£716,000) to the Vanderbilt University Medical Centre which helped to fund the Moderna vaccine.

2020年，当Covid-19大流行席卷全球时，帕顿向范德比尔特大学医学中心捐赠了100万美元（约合71.6万英镑），帮助资助了Moderna疫苗的研发。

In later years, Parton had to postpone or cancel several shows for health treatment.

晚年，帕顿因接受健康治疗而不得不推迟或取消了几场演出。

In May 2026, she postponed an upcoming Las Vegas residency because of treatment for kidney stones, saying it would take some time to heal and be "stage-performance ready".

2026年5月，她因为接受肾结石治疗而推迟了即将到来的拉斯维加斯驻唱演出，表示需要一些时间来恢复并达到“适合登台表演的状态”。

"I can't be dizzy carrying around banjos, guitars and such on five-inch heels - and you know that I'm going to be wearing them," she said in a video message.

她在一段视频信息中说：“我不能踩着五英寸高的高跟鞋，晕头转向地拿着班卓琴、吉他之类的东西——而且你知道我是肯定要穿它们的。”

"Not to mention all those heavy rhinestone outfits, the big hair, my big... uh, personality," she joked.

“更不用说那些沉重的水钻服装、夸张的发型，还有我巨大的……呃，个性，”她开玩笑说。

Parton spoke graciously throughout her career with humour and intelligence. She steered clear of politics, saying she didn't want to alienate half her audience.

在她的整个职业生涯中，帕顿始终保持优雅、幽默和智慧。她避开政治，表示不想疏远她一半的观众。

She was a courageous and talented trailblazer for country music at a time when women were not taken seriously in the industry, especially as solo artists.

在那个女性在行业中不被认真对待的时代，尤其是作为独唱艺术家，她是一位勇敢而才华横溢的乡村音乐先驱。

Parton said she hoped to be remembered for her songwriting and as somebody who took her work seriously, but not herself.

帕顿说，她希望人们因为她的词曲创作以及她是一个认真对待工作但不把自己太当回事的人而被铭记。

She once offered this advice: “Never ignore your roots, your home, or your hair."

她曾给出这样的建议：“永远不要忽视你的根、你的家，或者你的头发。”

It's guidance that she stayed true to throughout her life.

这也是她一生坚守的准则。
