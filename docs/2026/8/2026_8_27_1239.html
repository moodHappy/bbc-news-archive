Japanese avant-garde artist Yayoi Kusama dies aged 97
日本前卫艺术家草间弥生去世，享年97岁

📅 2026-08-27 12:36 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

Japanese avant-garde artist Yayoi Kusama, who became famous later in life for her work in sculptures and installations, has died aged 97, her company has confirmed.

据其公司证实，因晚年的雕塑和装置艺术作品而名声大噪的日本前卫艺术家草间弥生去世，享年97岁。

The statement announced, "with profound sorrow... the passing of Yayoi Kusama at a hospital in Tokyo on August 14, 2026, at the age of 97".

声明宣布，“我们怀着无比悲痛的心情……宣布草间弥生于2026年8月14日在东京的一家医院去世，享年97岁。”

"Throughout an extraordinary lifetime devoted entirely to artistic creation, Kusama pursued an internationally acclaimed career as a pioneering avant-garde artist whose creative practice encompassed visual art, performance, fiction, and poetry," it said.

声明说：“草间弥生将非凡的一生完全奉献给了艺术创作，作为一名开创性的前卫艺术家，她追求着享有国际盛誉的职业生涯，其创作实践涵盖了视觉艺术、行为艺术、小说和诗歌。”

Kusama, who had voluntarily lived in a pyschiatric hospital for decades, was known for her bright-red wig and quirky polka-dot ensembles.

几十年来一直自愿住在精神病院的草间弥生，以其鲜红的假发和古怪的波点服装而闻名。

"She continued to paint until her final days, never setting aside her brush, and continued exploration of forever love and eternal soul.

“直到生命的最后岁月，她仍在继续作画，从未放下画笔，并不断探索永恒的爱和不朽的灵魂。”

"We will carry forward Kusama's wishes and, through art, continue to bring hope and strength for the future to people around the world."

“我们将继承草间弥生的遗愿，通过艺术，继续为全世界的人们带来面向未来的希望和力量。”

Kusama became one of the world's most recognisable artists and a global cultural icon in her 80s and 90s, after having been mostly overlooked for decades.

在被忽视了几十年之后，草间弥生在八九十岁时成为了世界上最受认可的艺术家之一和全球文化偶像。

Poorly received by a masculine-dominated art scene of New York in the 1960s, Kusama caused a stir with her exhibitions of phallic sculptures, including a fabric couch, and frequent displays of nudity.

20世纪60年代，在由男性主导的纽约艺术圈里，草间弥生并未受到好评，但她展出的阳具雕塑（包括一张布艺沙发）以及频繁展示裸体的行为引起了轰动。

In 1965, her work "Infinity Mirror Rooms", in which mirrors and lights create seemingly endless spaces, attracted millions of visitors. Later, her polka-dot and pumpkin works became highly sought-after.

1965年，她的作品《无限镜屋》（Infinity Mirror Rooms）——利用镜子和灯光创造出看似无尽的空间——吸引了数百万游客。后来，她的波点和南瓜作品变得备受追捧。

She didn't attract international attention until decades later, going on to become Japan's first solo artist to represent the country at the 1993 Venice Biennale.

直到几十年后，她才引起国际关注，随后成为第一位代表日本参加1993年威尼斯双年展的本土个人艺术家。

The roaring success of the exhibition led to a huge transformation in how she was received both in Japan and abroad.

展览的巨大成功使她在日本国内外的受关注度发生了天翻地覆的改变。

One of her paintings fetched about $10.5m (£7.7m) at auction in 2022.

2022年，她的一幅画作在拍卖会上拍出了约1050万美元（770万英镑）的高价。

Then, her work appeared in major museums and collaborations with luxury brands including Louis Vuitton.

随后，她的作品登上了各大博物馆，并与包括路易威登（Louis Vuitton）在内的奢侈品牌展开了合作。

As a child, Kusama experienced hallucinations of embers and flashing – later using her art as a vehicle for others to understand her vision of the world.

小时候，草间弥生曾经历过看到余烬和闪光的幻觉——后来她把艺术作为一种媒介，让别人理解她眼中的世界。

Kusama was born in 1929 in the rural provincial town of Matsumoto, based in the central Nagano region of Japan.

1929年，草间弥生出生于日本中部地区长野县的乡村小镇松本市。

Her parents did not support her early moves to study painting in Kyoto.

她早年去京都学习绘画的举动并没有得到父母的支持。

She was later told that her artistry caused great shame to her conservative family. Some elements of the US press also criticised what they perceived as an endless desire for publicity.

后来有人告诉她，她的艺术天赋给保守的家庭带来了极大的耻辱。美国部分媒体也批评了他们眼中她对出名的无休止的渴望。

The world, along with her native Japan, radically changed its perception of Kusama in her later life.

晚年时期，全世界以及她的祖国日本彻底改变了对草间弥生的看法。

Social media fuelled a spike in interest over recent years – with her museum's bold floral shapes and shiny steel orbs turning into a desired backdrop for selfies and posing.

近年来，社交媒体极大地助推了人们对她的兴趣——她美术馆里大胆的花朵造型和闪亮的钢球成为了自拍和摆拍的热门背景。

Among her many motifs, the pumpkin has become iconic in her works. To her, the vegetable represented comfort and nostalgia.

在她的众多主题中，南瓜已成为她作品中的标志。对她来说，这种蔬菜代表着慰藉与怀旧。

She once said in an interview, "Pumpkins speak to me."

她曾在一次采访中说：“南瓜会和我说话。”

"They embody a sense of stability and gives me peace of mind."

“它们体现了一种稳定感，让我的内心感到平静。”

In her hometown of Naoshima, visitors coming by ferry are greeted by her outdoor Red Pumpkin sculpture covered in bold, black polka dots.

在她的家乡直岛（译注：原作者笔误，草间弥生出生于松本，直岛为其重要作品所在地），乘渡轮前来的游客会受到她那布满醒目黑色波点的户外《红南瓜》雕塑的迎接。
